About EduConnect

"EduConnect" is a modern tutoring platform developed as a final diploma project. Our mission is to streamline the interaction between students and educators, providing a comprehensive toolkit for effective learning and professional development.

Our Story

The project was initiated in 2026 at the "Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute (KPI)". The concept emerged from the necessity to create a unified digital environment for finding qualified mentors, where students can receive support in mastering complex technical disciplines.

Development Team

The platform was built by a dedicated developer, combining expertise in Python, Django, and modern web architecture:

  • "Viktor Dudchenko" — Lead Backend Developer / System Architect
  • " TR-25 Community" — Frontend Support & UI/UX Design

Our Core Principles

We believe that high-quality education should be accessible to everyone. EduConnect focuses on three key pillars:

  • "Accessibility:" Finding tutors for various learning formats, including Online and Offline sessions.
  • "Transparency:" A robust feedback and rating system to ensure the highest standards of teaching.
  • "Reliability:" Utilizing Docker and cloud-based solutions to ensure stable and scalable service performance.
